AutoMix: transitions that listen

AutoMix analyses your songs and chooses a transition that suits them. When the rhythm allows, it lines up the beats by gliding the tempo gradually, without changing the pitch; when two songs don't suit each other it picks another kind of transition instead.

  • Finds each song's tempo, beats and bars on your device.
  • Mixes in time, bar by bar, with a bass swap and a filter sweep.
  • Falls back to a simple crossfade when a mix wouldn't sound right.
  • Choose short, normal or long mixes, or switch it off.

AutoMix is a beta feature: it works, but it is still being tuned, and some transitions may not sound right yet.

Beta Premium

Playlist Mix: design every transition

Open a playlist of downloaded songs and shape each transition yourself, with waveforms to see exactly where the songs meet.

  • Pick the length in bars, or let Auto choose a preset: Fade, Rise, Blur and more.
  • Draw the volume curves, swap the bass, add a filter or an effect.
  • Listen to the transition before you save it.

The playlist Mix is a beta feature too.

Follow along, then sing

Synced lyrics scroll with the song and jump to a line when you tap it. Translate them with on-device tools, and let AI karaoke take the voice out of the song: it all happens on your device.

  • Synced lyrics from LRCLIB, with automatic scrolling.
  • Translation where your device supports the language.
  • AI karaoke: slide the vocals down to silence, or keep them and lose the band.
  • Recognise music playing nearby with one tap.

Sound that's yours

Choose the audio quality for Wi-Fi, mobile data and downloads. Maximum quality can use Opus; Dolby Digital Plus or AAC when they're available. The format depends on the song and your device.

  • Equaliser with presets, or a curve of your own.
  • Volume normalisation, so songs sit at the same level.
  • Controls on the Lock Screen, in Control Center, over AirPlay and Bluetooth.
  • Bit-perfect output to USB DACs on Android 14 and later.

Install Seaglass

Seaglass isn't on the App Store or Google Play: it comes straight from GitHub.

iPhone & iPad

iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. The IPA is unsigned: sign and install it with Sideloadly, AltStore Classic or SideStore.

  1. Download Seaglass.ipa.
  2. Open it in your sideloading tool and follow its steps.
  3. If asked, turn on Developer Mode and trust the profile in Settings.

Mac

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) on macOS 26 Tahoe or later.

  1. Download and open Seaglass-mac.zip.
  2. Drag Seaglass to Applications.
  3. The first time, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. Updates install over the app and keep your library.

Questions

Why is AutoMix called beta?

It's new and still being tuned on many kinds of music: the choice of transition and its timing can still be off for some songs. The playlist Mix is the same. They improve with every version.

Does AutoMix always beat-match?

No. It favours transitions in time when they suit the songs, but picks another transition when their rhythms or vocals would make a mix sound wrong.

Why isn't the audio always Opus?

What's available varies by song. The app may use another format, for example when Dolby is available or Opus can't be decoded. The player shows the format, and the settings have diagnostics.

Why are some songs missing after an import?

Some recordings can't be found or matched with enough confidence. The import report lists them and offers alternatives. For long Spotify playlists, import a JSON or CSV file.

How do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub with your app version, your device and the steps to reproduce it. Settings → Diagnostics can build a report.
