# First-listen feedback

Use this short script with people who have agreed to try Seaglass. Ask them to share their experience in their own words; do not ask for passwords, tokens or account screenshots.

## Invitation draft

> I'm testing Seaglass, a music player for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android, and I'd appreciate honest feedback. Would you be willing to try the installation and a few minutes of listening? Some features on Apple devices require Premium, and iPhone/iPad installation uses sideloading. Here are the downloads and requirements: https://github.com/Foglio24/onda#installation

## Observe the first attempt

1. What did you expect Seaglass to do? What caught your attention?
2. Could you find the download and understand the requirements for your device?
3. Where, if anywhere, did installation become confusing? What device and OS version were you using?
4. Could you find and play a song? How did playback, lyrics and the player feel?
5. Did you understand which features are free and which require Premium on Apple devices?
6. If you tried AutoMix or Playlist Mix, how did the transitions sound? Both are beta features.
7. What would make you use Seaglass again? What would stop you recommending it?

For each response, note the device and OS, the step attempted, what happened, and whether it prevented installation or listening. Ask for a screenshot only when helpful and remind the tester to remove personal information. Keep feedback private unless they agree to share it.

## Traffic and measurement

GitHub repository owners can consult **Insights → Traffic** for page views, unique visitors, clones and referring sites over GitHub's available reporting window. Release asset download counts are separate and are not unique installations or active users. Compare traffic and downloads over the same dates; neither number alone establishes why someone did or did not install.
